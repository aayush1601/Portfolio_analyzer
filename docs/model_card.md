# Model card: expected-return models (Phase 9)

_Generated by `app.services.ml` on 2026-10-04 17:38 UTC. Re-run to refresh; do not edit by hand._

## Purpose
Rank the NIFTY 50 universe by expected 5, 20-day return relative to the cross-sectional average,
as one input (`expected_return`) to the recommendation engine. Research output, not financial advice.

## Data
- Prices: NSE daily closes (Yahoo, split- and dividend-adjusted) 2015-01-01 to 2026-10-01; 126,097 labelled stock-days.
- News: FinBERT-scored RSS headlines from 2024-04-23 (8 trading days with any news).
- Universe: today's NIFTY 50 constituents (survivorship bias: backtests are flattered; see universe.yaml).

## Features (point-in-time, known by 15:30 IST on the feature date)
Market: ret_1d, ret_5d, ret_20d, ret_60d, rel_ret_20d, vol_20d, vol_60d, dist_52w_high, volume_z_20d, beta_60d, mkt_ret_5d, mkt_ret_20d, vix, vix_chg_5d.
News: news_count_7d, news_score_3d, news_score_7d, news_neg_share_7d (articles published after the close count toward the next trading day).

## Target
Forward return over h trading days minus that date's mean across the universe (what a ranking can earn).

## Method
Expanding-window walk-forward, one test year at a time from 2019, with an h-day purge gap before each
test year. Models: historical mean (baseline), ridge regression, random forest, XGBoost. Metrics: mean daily
rank IC (Spearman), its t-stat on non-overlapping dates (every h-th), hit rate (sign of predicted vs realised
excess return), share of dates with positive IC.
